Unhinged Q&A — with cocktails & hors d'oeuvres

Join Dr. Steven Bishop, Dr. Bo Vaughan, and fellow Valiant members at the Commonwealth Club for an evening of connection, curiosity, and honest conversation about health and wellness.

The centerpiece is an Unhinged Q&A — hosted and moderated by two Valiant patients who will bring the most interesting, curiosity-inducing questions about health and wellness from the social media world. Dr. Bishop and Dr. Vaughan will answer them live.

Cocktails and hors d'oeuvres will be served throughout the evening. Come as you are, bring a guest if you'd like, and settle in for a few hours built for connection — not clinics.

No lectures. No sales pitch. Just sharp questions, real answers, and time with the people who know your health story.
